# Credits and licences

## Photography and video

All photographs and videos are Pat Premier Roofing and Building's own job
photos and TikTok videos, supplied by the business. Photos were resized to
1600px and saved without EXIF/location data. Videos were compressed to 480px
and their soundtracks removed (TikTok soundtracks are often licensed music).

`public/brand/logo-on-light.svg` and `logo-on-dark.svg` are vector traces of the
Pat Premier logo from the company's supplied promotional banner.
