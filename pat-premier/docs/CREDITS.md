# Credits and licences

## Demonstration photography

All files in `src/assets/demo/` are royalty-free photographs from Unsplash
(Unsplash Licence: free for commercial use, no attribution required). Each
image's source ID is recorded in `src/data/media.ts`. They are placeholders
only, labelled on the site, and must be replaced with Pat Premier's own photos.

## Logo

`public/brand/logo-on-light.svg` and `logo-on-dark.svg` are vector traces of the
Pat Premier logo from the company's supplied promotional banner (the window is
redrawn as clean geometry). Colours follow the original navy and royal blue.
