A living room transformed with a built-in media wall and a new floor.

A timber stud frame was built across the wall and set out for the TV recess, display niches and shelving, with cable routes planned in. It was then boarded, with corner beads on every edge ready for plastering.

The floor was prepared, foil-faced underlay laid, and grey oak-effect water-resistant laminate fitted across the room.
