Rotten timber at the eaves is a common hidden problem: once the fascia fails, water gets into the ends of the rafters and the gutter loses its support.

Here the old fascia and the decayed rafter ends at the corner were cut back and rebuilt in new timber, then new fascia boards were fixed along the eaves, giving the gutters a sound edge to hang from again.
